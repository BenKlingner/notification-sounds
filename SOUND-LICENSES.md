[Envato Elements](https://elements.envato.com). Each item was licensed by Ben Klingner on October 25, 2026
under a single Envato Elements license registered to the project "Notifcation Sounds for Paseo" (the
project name is spelled that way on the certificates). The author retains the license certificates privately, one PDF per item, named by license code.
They are not included in the public repository or shipped with the plugin.

An Envato Elements license is non-exclusive, commercial, and worldwide, and covers use of the item as
part of one end product. Notification sounds is that end product. The license does not pass through to
users of the plugin: the bundled recordings may be used only as part of Notification sounds and may not be
extracted, redistributed on their own, or reused in another project. Attribution is not required by the
license, but the authors are credited below.

The sounds are embedded as trimmed, mono MP3 copies of the licensed WAV files. The label column is the
name shown in the plugin; the item column is the title on Envato Elements, linked to the item page.

## How the mapping was made

Each certificate's item title was matched to the name of the Envato download folder the sound came from,
as recorded by the Sound Librarian analysis report. Where several licensed items share a title (Done,
Finish, Task Completed, Alert, Message Sent, Message Sent 1, Process Complete, Space Impact, Good Idea),
the clip length shown on the Envato item page was compared with the length of the downloaded file.
